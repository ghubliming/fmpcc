# Supplementary data

Supplementary material of the thesis *Flow Matching Predictive Control with Constraints* (Liming Liu, Technical University of Munich, 2026).
Datasets and trained weights are not kept here.

- `Appendix_B_Extended_Results_online.pdf` — the online part of Appendix B, *Extended Results*: Sections B.6, B.8 and B.9 with their
  tables, typeset as in the thesis and numbered as there.
- More data, the raw records included, is available from the author on personal request.
